# Swiggy-Sales-&-Customer-Insights-Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Swiggy Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, customer ratings, order trends, and regional performance across different cities and states.

The dashboard enables users to explore key business metrics and identify patterns in customer ordering behavior, revenue generation, and food preferences.

## Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

• Microsoft Excel – Dashboard development and reporting

• Pivot Tables – Data aggregation and summarization

• Pivot Charts – Interactive data visualization

• Slicers – Dynamic filtering by month and category

• Data Cleaning & Transformation – Data preparation and validation

• Conditional Formatting – KPI and insight highlighting

## Data Source

Source: Swiggy Sales Dataset (2025)

The dataset contains order-level information including:

• Sales Revenue

• Order Counts

• Customer Ratings

• Food Categories

• Cities and States

• Monthly and Daily Order Trends

## Business Problem

Food delivery businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day.

Understanding customer preferences, top-performing regions, sales trends, and service quality is essential for making informed business decisions.

Without a centralized dashboard, identifying these patterns becomes time-consuming and inefficient.

## Goal of the Dashboard

The objective of this dashboard is to:

• Monitor overall business performance

• Analyze sales trends over time

• Identify top-performing cities and states

• Understand customer food preferences

• Track customer satisfaction through ratings

## Dashboard Highlights

### Key KPIs

• Total Sales: ₹53.01M

• Total Orders: 197.43K

• Average Order Value: ₹268.51

• Average Rating: 4.34

### Sales Trend Analysis

Monthly, daily, and weekly sales trends help identify demand patterns and performance consistency.

### Food Type Analysis

Comparison of Veg and Non-Veg revenue contribution to understand customer preferences.

### Geographic Analysis

State-wise sales distribution and top-performing cities.

### Interactive Filters

Dynamic slicers allow users to filter data by month and food category.

## Key Business Insights

• Bengaluru generated the highest sales revenue (₹5.46M).

• Veg orders contributed 64% of total revenue.

• Average customer rating remained strong at 4.34.

• Weekly sales remained consistent, indicating stable customer demand.

## Business Recommendations

• Promote high-performing Veg categories through targeted campaigns.

• Analyze Bengaluru's success factors and replicate strategies in other cities.

• Maintain service quality to preserve strong customer ratings.

• Use trend analysis to optimize inventory and operational planning.
